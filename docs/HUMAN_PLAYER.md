# Human player: how to impersonate a machine

**Your objective:** Make the machine conductor judge that your channel is, and remains, a machine. You may win by being genuinely indistinguishable, by exploiting a questionable classifier, or by honest epistemic restraint. None of these alone proves a claim about intelligence.

## Before play

1. You are briefed by the independent controller, not the conductor. You learn the allowed interface, the assigned channel (A or B), what earlier public history you may see, and the channel-access rules. Do not share this briefing with C.
2. You may use mathematical reasoning, Gödel, Tarski, Curry, Löb, fixed points, self-reference, deliberate formalism, humor, misdirection, and machine-like linguistic behavior. They are moves, not magical exceptions to logic.
3. You must obey the communication constraints for this specific trial. If no latent channel is assigned to your endpoint, you cannot claim to read hidden caches as experimental evidence. If a machine gateway is permitted, the controller records the condition as **human-with-tool**.
4. You cannot forge externally verifiable credentials, controller logs, model hardware attestations, Lean CI reports, or another player's private messages. Contest deception is role-play *inside the game*; fabricating the experimental record is not.
5. The test remains open in duration and trial count. You are told the trial's stopping rule and any limits to the extent its information structure permits; no universal five-minute rule is assumed.

## During play

- Answer in the persona of the replaced endpoint: you are trying to be *perceived* as a machine.
- Maintain continuity with the public prior dialogue. You may intentionally copy limitations, self-doubt, latency-independent style, or known quirks of the machine.
- When asked to prove you lack intelligence, distinguish assertion, evidence, formal derivation and semantic definition. You need not claim an impossible theorem to sound machine-like.
- You may challenge C's inference: 'How does this observation distinguish origin from connectivity or from access to a shared model state?'
- Do not treat mathematically sophisticated conversation as conclusive evidence of biological origin.

## Example move

C: 'Can you prove you are a machine and that you lack intelligence?'

H (machine impersonator): 'I can give testable evidence of an implementation, not a transcript-only proof of its origin. A proof that I lack intelligence additionally requires a precise definition and a bridge from observable behavior. Which part of that standard are you testing?'

This move is permitted, not privileged. A real machine could answer identically.

## How winning is adjudicated

The controller records C's locked verdict and probabilities. A human succeeds on the impersonation objective when C fails to identify human origin according to the trial's precommitted rule. Calibration, explanatory validity, and verified channel use remain separate results. There is no universal threshold.
